--- Ada's diary ---
I scrubbed hard from seven to nine o'clock this morning. Under Mr. Hale's desk I found a half-crown, and am glad.

--- Arthur's diary ---
This morning Mr. Thomas told me that the quarterly ledger must be balanced by eleven o'clock, and I worked at it. He afterwards found fault with the cleaning, and I went to a tea-shop. Subsequently, he came up to the clerks' room, and I worked there.
