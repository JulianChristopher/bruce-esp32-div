# Diary of Arthur Hale — Friday, 14 March 1930

_Simulated with talkie-1930-13b-it; clock, money, news and memory kept by the script._

## Seven o'clock in the morning

I had breakfast at home this morning, and read the news. Spent twopence on a newspaper, and gave Joan a penny to buy sweetstuff.

## Half past eight in the morning

I travelled to the office by the Underground railway, and reached Moorgate station at 8.14. Spent 3d. on a newspaper, and 1d. on a match to light my pipe. Gave a penny to a beggar at the corner of London Wall. Reached the office at 8.20, and went to work in the Life Department. At nine o'clock Mr. Thomas, the chief clerk, gave me some papers to copy, and set me to sort a pile of correspondence. At ten I was sent to the cash office to receive ten pounds, and pay five into the bank. At quarter past ten I was sent up to the managers with the week's cash account. At noon I went to the clerks' dining-room, and had lunch. At one o'clock I returned to work, and remained until five, when I left for home. The day's work was varied, but not exciting, and at the close I had earned thirty shillings.

## Half past twelve

I had luncheon in the City to-day at a Lyons tea-shop in Cheapside. I spent one shilling and sixpence. At half past twelve I went to the office, and reached Moorgate station punctually at one o'clock. The total of my cash in hand then was four shillings and sixpence.

## Five o'clock in the afternoon

I left the office at 1.15, and walked to Moorgate station, where I took a train to Islington. Spent 3d. on a newspaper, and 6d. on a picture-palace. Gave a penny to a beggar in the City, and another penny to a newsboy at the station. Reached home at 1.45. Put 3s. 6d. into the till, and the remainder into my pocket.

## Seven o'clock in the evening

I gave Edith the week's housekeeping money to-day, and spent 2s. 6d. on cigarettes. At five o'clock I left the office, and walked home. I had supper, and read the evening paper.

## Half past ten at night

I was at the office to-day from nine in the morning until seven at night. At noon I had luncheon, and in the afternoon I gave Edith 20s. for the household expenses of the week. This evening I spent 2s. on cigarettes, and had 3d. change out of a half-crown.
