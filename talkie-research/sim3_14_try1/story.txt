--- Mrs. Ada Turner's diary, Friday, the 14th of March, 1930 (3/3 key facts kept) ---
I found a half-crown under Mr. Hale's desk, and was pleased. Mr. Thomas paid me my week's wages, and I was pleased. Bert gave me his share of the rent, and that pleased me too.
   ◇ Arthur reflects: He thinks Mrs. Edith Hale a fine woman.
   ◇ Arthur reflects: Mr. Hale thinks Mr. Thomas a steady, respectable man of business.

--- Mr. Arthur Hale's diary, Friday, the 14th of March, 1930 (1/2 key facts kept) ---
I spoke with Edith in the morning, and searched for the half-crown on the floor, but could not find it. I spoke with Mr. Thomas about the work in hand, and he thanked me, and I thanked him. In the evening I spoke to him about the news in the paper, and he replied that it was a fine day.
   ◇ Mr. Thomas reflects: He thinks Mr. Arthur Hale a civil young man.

--- Mr. Walter Thomas's diary, Friday, the 14th of March, 1930 (0/1 key facts kept) ---
I spoke with Mr. Arthur Hale about the work in hand and thanked him. I also spoke with him about the news in the paper, and remarked upon the fineness of the day.
